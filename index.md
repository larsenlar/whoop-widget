# Privacy Policy – Larsen WHOOP Rings

**Last updated:** November 24, 2026

Larsen WHOOP Rings is a personal fitness dashboard created for private use.

### Information collected
The application accesses WHOOP fitness data, including:
- Daily Strain
- Recovery scores
- Sleep information
- Workout information

It may also access Apple Health activity information, including active energy and step counts.

### How information is used
Information is used exclusively to display personal fitness metrics in a private iPhone widget.

### Data storage
WHOOP authentication credentials and tokens are stored locally on the user's device. Fitness data is used for personal display and is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

### Third-party services
The application uses the WHOOP API and Apple Health integrations, subject to their respective privacy policies.
